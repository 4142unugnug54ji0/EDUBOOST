📖 Quarter 3 Week 5 - Learning Experience

📝 Describing Words

Describing words tell us more about a person, place, or thing.
They describe how something looks or feels.

Examples: hard window, bold friends.

💬 ASK & SAY

ASK: What are describing words?
SAY: They tell more about a noun.

ASK: Give an example.
SAY: Hard window and bold friends.

🔤 CVCC Words

CVCC words follow the pattern consonant-vowel-consonant-consonant.
Examples: left
milk
sand
belt

💬 ASK & SAY

ASK: What are CVCC words?
SAY: Words with consonant-vowel-consonant-consonant pattern.

ASK: Give an example.
SAY: Left, milk, and sand.

📖 Using Describing Words

Describing words make sentences clearer.

Example:
She sifts the firm grain.

The word "firm" describes the grain.

💬 ASK & SAY

ASK: Why do we use describing words?
SAY: To make sentences clear and detailed.

ASK: What is the describing word in the sentence?
SAY: Firm.
