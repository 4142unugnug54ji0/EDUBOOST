📖 Quarter 4 Week 7 - Learning Experience

🐝 The World Needs Bees

Bees are very important. They help plants grow.
Bees live in hives. Some bees work together, while others live alone.
Bees help plants by spreading pollen. Because of bees, we can have fruits, seeds, and flowers.
Without bees, many plants will not grow well.

💬 ASK & SAY

ASK: Why are bees important?
SAY: They help plants grow.
ASK: Where do bees live?
SAY: In hives.
ASK: What happens without bees?
SAY: Plants will not grow well.

🧪 Procedure (Bottled Music)

We follow steps to do something.
First, prepare bottles, water, and coloring.
Next, fill the bottles with water.
Then, add food coloring.
After that, test the sound.
Finally, play music.

💬 ASK & SAY

ASK: What word tells the first step?
SAY: First.
ASK: What comes after next?
SAY: Then.
ASK: Why do we use these words?
SAY: To show order.

🌞 Summer

Summer is a fun season.
The weather is warm and sunny.
People go to the beach and swim.

🌍 Climate Change

Climate change affects our Earth.
It can make weather hotter.
Ice can melt and turn into water.
Storms can become stronger.

We can help by planting trees and saving energy.

💬 ASK & SAY

ASK: What is climate change?
SAY: It changes the weather.
ASK: What happens to ice?
SAY: It melts.
ASK: How can we help?
SAY: Plant trees.
